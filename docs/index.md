# Codex: Math & Physics Textbook Solutions

Welcome to **Codex**, an open-source, curated collection of step-by-step solutions to exercises and problems from standard mathematics and physics textbooks.

---

## Mission

1. **Rigor & Clarity**: Solutions provide complete mathematical steps, physical motivation, boundary-case checks, and dimensional analysis.
2. **Standardized Formatting**: Powered by Markdown and LaTeX ($...$ and $$...$$), readable both in browser via GitHub Pages and directly inside the repository.
3. **Targeted Computational Verification**: Where problems explicitly call for numerical calculation, orbit/trajectory simulation, or plotting, clean Python scripts and visualizations are provided inline.
