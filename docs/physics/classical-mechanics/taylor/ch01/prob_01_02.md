## Problem Statement

Two vectors are given in Cartesian components:

$$
\mathbf{b} = (1, 2, 3), \quad \mathbf{c} = (3, 2, 1)
$$

Find:

1. $\mathbf{b} + \mathbf{c}$
2. $5\mathbf{b} - 2\mathbf{c}$
3. $\mathbf{b} \cdot \mathbf{c}$
4. $\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c}$

---

## Solution

### Part 1: Vector Addition $\mathbf{b} + \mathbf{c}$

Adding the vectors component-wise:

$$
\mathbf{b} + \mathbf{c} = (b_x + c_x, \, b_y + c_y, \, b_z + c_z)
$$

$$
\mathbf{b} + \mathbf{c} = (1 + 3, \, 2 + 2, \, 3 + 1) = (4, 4, 4)
$$

---

### Part 2: Linear Combination $5\mathbf{b} - 2\mathbf{c}$

Scaling each vector by its scalar coefficient:

$$
5\mathbf{b} = 5(1, 2, 3) = (5, 10, 15)
$$

$$
2\mathbf{c} = 2(3, 2, 1) = (6, 4, 2)
$$

Subtracting corresponding components:

$$
5\mathbf{b} - 2\mathbf{c} = (5 - 6, \, 10 - 4, \, 15 - 2) = (-1, 6, 13)
$$

---

### Part 3: Scalar (Dot) Product $\mathbf{b} \cdot \mathbf{c}$

The scalar product is computed by summing the products of corresponding components:

$$
\mathbf{b} \cdot \mathbf{c} = b_x c_x + b_y c_y + b_z c_z
$$

$$
\mathbf{b} \cdot \mathbf{c} = (1)(3) + (2)(2) + (3)(1) = 3 + 4 + 3 = 10
$$

---

### Part 4: Vector (Cross) Product $\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c}$

The cross product in three dimensions is given by the determinant:

$$
\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c} = \begin{vmatrix}
\hat{\mathbf{x}} & \hat{\mathbf{y}} & \hat{\mathbf{z}} \\
b_x & b_y & b_z \\
c_x & c_y & c_z
\end{vmatrix} = \begin{vmatrix}
\hat{\mathbf{x}} & \hat{\mathbf{y}} & \hat{\mathbf{z}} \\
1 & 2 & 3 \\
3 & 2 & 1
\end{vmatrix}
$$

Expanding by cofactors across the top row:

$$
\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c} = \hat{\mathbf{x}} \left( (2)(1) - (3)(2) \right) - \hat{\mathbf{y}} \left( (1)(1) - (3)(3) \right) + \hat{\mathbf{z}} \left( (1)(2) - (2)(3) \right)
$$

$$
\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c} = \hat{\mathbf{x}}(2 - 6) - \hat{\mathbf{y}}(1 - 9) + \hat{\mathbf{z}}(2 - 6)
$$

$$
\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c} = -4\hat{\mathbf{x}} + 8\hat{\mathbf{y}} - 4\hat{\mathbf{z}} = (-4, 8, -4)
$$
